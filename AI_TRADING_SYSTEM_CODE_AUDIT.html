2.2 Confidence Calculation

confidence = 0.28 × trendStrength 
           + 0.28 × marketHealth 
           + 0.30 × entryQuality 
           + 0.14 × (100 - reversalProbability)

2.3 Signal Generation

  • Strong Buy: Bullish trend, confidence > 85, strength > 75
  • Buy: Bullish trend, confidence > 70, strength > 55
  • Neutral: No clear direction
  • Sell/Strong Sell: Inverse of buy conditions

3. Trade Setup Engine

3.1 Trade Quality Calculation

tradeQuality = 0.45 × ratioQuality 
             + 0.35 × entryQuality 
             + 0.20 × riskQuality

3.2 Stop Loss Generation

FOR Long Position:
    stopLoss = entryPrice - (ATR × 1.5)

FOR Short Position:
    stopLoss = entryPrice + (ATR × 1.5)

3.3 Take Profit Levels

TP1 = entry + (risk × 2)
TP2 = entry + (risk × 3)

3.4 Position Sizing
